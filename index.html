Hi, I am Huang Qiming

I am a PhD student at the National University of Singapore (NUS), working with Prof. Mike Zheng Shou in Show Lab.

My research interests include AI agents, unified models, and vision-language-action (VLA) models. My recent work explores how vision-language models can control robots, how synthetic videos can support robot learning, and how to segment robots in images and videos.

I am also an intern at Microsoft. Before starting my PhD in 2025, I studied at NUS as an undergraduate from 2021 to 2025.

You can find my work on the publications page and more about my background in my CV.
